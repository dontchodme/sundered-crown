Where viewers actually leave

Per-second survival curves for eight TikTok posts, read from TikTok's own analytics API on 19 Aug 2026. Left: the cold open's entire effect is spent in seconds 1–3, and it is large. Right: once a viewer reaches 0:05, the curves collapse onto each other — cold open and baseline decay at the same rate, and so do eight very different fights.

The shaded strip is seconds 1–3. The cold open cuts the loss in second 2 from 0.30 to 0.16 — a 47% smaller cliff — and every advantage it holds for the rest of the video was bought right there.

Of the viewers who reach 0:05, who is left

The same curves, re-based so everyone starts at 100% at 0:05. Cold-open mean tail 0.436, baseline 0.429 — a 1.6% difference, and the two groups interleave completely in the ranking.

One caveat on the source. The area under each curve runs 11–20% below the average watch time TikTok reports for the same video, consistently and in the same direction for all eight. The most likely reason is that reported watch time counts loops while the curve is capped at 100% at t=0 — but that is a hypothesis, not something checked. The shape is what this chart is about, and the shape ranks the eight videos in the same order as the reported averages, so the bias does not move any conclusion here.
