**THE DOUBLE REFLECTION.** §5 names `src.ultShroud` as the guard; the build holds a flag (`_reflecting`) while a reflected blow resolves. That is §5's intent in its own words ("a reflected blow does NOT reflect again") — its named guard would ALSO silence every ordinary blow while both fighters are shrouded, which it does not ask for. Only a mirror match tells them apart, and **the engine refuses a mirror** ("A relic cannot fight itself"), so the probe stages one (a synthetic shroud on the foe): 12 staged mirror fights, 275 reflections, 0 nested.

**THE TICKS (open decision 4).** §5: "Damage-over-time ticks count ... measured in". On this engine a status tick does not come through `hurt()` (`tickStatus` debits hp directly) and is a fraction of a point a step, so round(tick × 0.5) is 0 — and the lab's per-frame watch rounded them away the same way unless a blow landed on the same frame. So as built, and as priced, a tick is not thrown back. Making ticks count means accumulating them — a design decision, and not Code's (rule 0).
